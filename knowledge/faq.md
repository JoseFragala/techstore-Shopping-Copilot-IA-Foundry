# TechStore FAQ

Q: What does TechStore sell?

A: TechStore sells computers, notebooks, monitors, keyboards, mice, and other technology products.

Q: Can I change my delivery address?

A: Customers should contact TechStore support as soon as possible. Address changes may not be possible after an order has been shipped.

Q: How can I check my order?

A: Customers can provide their order number to TechStore support so the order status can be checked.

Q: Does TechStore have physical stores?

A: TechStore is a fictional online-only e-commerce company used for this demonstration project.
