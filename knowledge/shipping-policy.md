# TechStore Shipping Policy

TechStore processes approved orders within one business day.

Orders approved before 3:00 PM are normally processed on the same business day.

Orders approved after 3:00 PM are processed on the next business day.

Estimated delivery times:

- São Paulo: 3 to 5 business days.
- Other Brazilian states: 5 to 10 business days.

Delivery estimates start after the order has been processed.

Delivery times may be affected by weekends, holidays, carrier delays, or incorrect customer address information.
