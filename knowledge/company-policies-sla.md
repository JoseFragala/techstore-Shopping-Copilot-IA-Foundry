# TechStore - Company Policies and Service Level Agreement (SLA)

This document consolidates TechStore's operational policies, customer service commitments, warranty guidelines, and Service Level Agreements (SLA) for customer support reference and transparent store operations.

---

## 1. Support Channels and Customer Service SLA

TechStore prioritizes prompt response and first-contact resolution. Our support channels and agreed response windows are:

| Support Channel | Availability | First Response Time (SLA) | Primary Scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AI Support Chat (TechStore AI)** | 24/7 (Continuous) | Immediate (< 5 seconds) | General inquiries, product catalog, policies, order status |
| **Human Support Specialist** | Mon - Fri, 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM | Within 15 minutes (in queue) | Complex inquiries, escalations, order cancellations |
| **Email Support (`support@techstore.com`)** | 24/7 receipt (Triage during business hours) | Within 4 business hours | Invoice corrections, warranty claims, returns |
| **Customer Support Phone** | Mon - Fri, 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM | Within 10 minutes | Urgent logistics and payment issues |

> **Escalation Note:** If a customer requests a human representative or if an issue involves payment disputes or exceptions, the AI assistant should clearly provide the human support hours or direct the user to email support.

## 2. Fulfillment, Shipping, and Delivery SLA

### Order Processing and Cut-off Time
- **Daily Processing Cut-off Time:** Orders with confirmed payment placed before **3:00 PM** are processed and dispatched on the **same business day**.
- Orders approved after 3:00 PM, on weekends, or on public holidays are processed and shipped on the **next business day**.
- **Tracking Number:** Automatically generated and emailed to the customer within **2 business hours** of carrier dispatch.

### Estimated Delivery Timelines
- **Metropolitan / Capital Areas:** 1 to 3 business days.
- **Regional / Major Cities:** 3 to 5 business days.
- **Remote / Other States:** 5 to 10 business days.

### Delivery Address Changes
- Delivery address updates are permitted only while the order is in the "Processing" stage.
- Once the invoice is generated and the shipment has been dispatched, address modifications cannot be made due to carrier and security restrictions.

## 3. Return, Exchange, and Refund Policy

### 7-Day Return Window (Right of Withdrawal)
- Customers can request a full or partial return within **7 calendar days** after receiving the shipment, no questions asked.
- Return shipping costs for eligible returns are **100% covered by TechStore** via prepaid carrier return labels.
- **Condition Requirements:** Items must be returned in their original packaging, complete with all included accessories, manuals, and cables, with no signs of accidental damage or misuse.

### Refund Processing Timelines (Financial SLA)
Once the returned package reaches our fulfillment center, our quality inspection team inspects the item within **3 business days**:
- **Instant Payment / Bank Transfer:** Refund completed within **24 business hours** after inspection approval.
- **Credit Card:** Cancellation submitted to the payment gateway within **2 business days**; credit reflects on the customer's card statement within 1 to 2 billing cycles depending on the issuing bank.

## 4. Warranty and Technical Support Policy

### Warranty Coverage
- **Standard Warranty:** All products include a standard 12-month manufacturer warranty covering hardware defects under normal operational use.
- **Extended Warranty:** Professional monitors and enterprise workstations carry up to 24 or 36 months of warranty, as listed on each product specification sheet.

### What the Warranty Covers
- Component failures, manufacturing defects, and spontaneous hardware malfunctions under normal intended use.

### What Voids the Warranty
- Liquid spill damage or submersion.
- Physical damage from drops, impacts, or excessive mechanical force.
- Electrical surges caused by non-certified power adapters or incorrect voltages.
- Unauthorized disassembly, repair attempts, or broken warranty seals.
- Normal aesthetic wear and tear.

### Technical Support SLA
- Warranty ticket review and triage within **24 business hours**.
- Standard repair or replacement turnaround time: **10 to 14 business days**.

## 5. Payment Methods and Terms

- **Upfront Instant Payment:** 5% instant discount applied at checkout.
- **Credit Card:** Up to 10 interest-free installments (minimum installment value: $50.00).
- **Invoices / Bank Slips:** 2-business-day due date; payment clearance in up to 48 hours.

---

## 6. Data Privacy and Security

- TechStore complies with standard customer data protection regulations (GDPR / LGPD).
- Credit card data is never stored on TechStore servers (processed securely via PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways).
- Customer information is strictly utilized for order fulfillment, tax invoicing, and direct communication.
